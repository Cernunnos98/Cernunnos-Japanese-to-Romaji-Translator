# NINJAL loanword survey attribution and reuse note

## 外来語定着度調査 / Survey of the Establishment of Loanwords

Publisher and copyright holder: National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics (NINJAL).
Survey period: 2002 to 2004.
Coverage stated by NINJAL: 405 loanwords, with recognition, understanding and usage results.
Results page: https://mmsrv.ninjal.ac.jp/gairaigo_yoron/
NINJAL resource index: https://www.ninjal.ac.jp/resources/search/

NINJAL's legacy loanword site states that NINJAL retains copyright. Citation is permitted with attribution; academic, educational and training reproduction/linking is permitted with attribution, while commercial reproduction requires prior contact with NINJAL. Accordingly, CJ2R does **not** redistribute the NINJAL survey dataset or copy survey rows into the runtime loanword bank.

CJ2R use: NINJAL is used as supplementary evidence for coverage-gap discovery and for checking whether candidate loanwords were established/recognised in Japanese. Survey presence never supplies the source-language spelling and never promotes a runtime mapping without independent authoritative spelling evidence.

## Modern NINJAL loanword research

NINJAL's language-problem project publishes newer research including the 2023 survey of contemporary loanword usage and the 2025 follow-up survey of loanword establishment. CJ2R records these as modern supplementary context only and does not redistribute their raw datasets.

Project page: https://www2.ninjal.ac.jp/lpdb/
