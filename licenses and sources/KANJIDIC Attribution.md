The translator includes compact Kanji helper data derived from **KANJIDIC2/KANJIDIC**, maintained by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG).

Project files covered by this attribution:

- `data/kanji/kanji-bank-1.json`
- `data/kanji/kanji-bank-2.json`
- `data/kanji/kanji-tag-bank-1.json`

Source metadata retained with the project identifies the reviewed snapshot as `KANJIDIC [2026-222]`, revision `kanjidic2.2026-222`, generated through the Yomitan import pipeline.

Official EDRDG project/licence information:

- https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project

EDRDG's general dictionary licence expressly covers KANJIDIC2, KANJIDIC and data derived from them under CC BY-SA 4.0, with additional source-specific conditions for some included KANJIDIC fields. The project retains the EDRDG and CC BY-SA notices in this directory.

The Kanji helper is not used to invent contextual word readings one character at a time. It remains supporting data for the manual Kanji-readings view and whole-word evidence workflows.
