# Japanese government data attribution

CJ2R uses Japanese government character-form material as reference evidence when reviewing old/new Japanese Kanji mappings in `data/kanji/kanji-variants.json`.

Primary reference: Agency for Cultural Affairs, Jōyō Kanji information:
https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/kanji/

The Agency for Cultural Affairs states that use of content on `bunka.go.jp` is governed by the Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) website terms. Those terms permit copying, public transmission, translation and adaptation, including commercial use, subject to attribution and the stated exceptions. MEXT also states that the terms are compatible with Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Agency website terms reference:
https://www.bunka.go.jp/bunkacho_homepage/
